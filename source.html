 646  define phrase valve-right
 647    You turn the valve right. A deep groan echoes through the pipes.
 648  
 649    The water surges in. It does not stop. It rises past your waist, your
 650    chest, your chin. The last thing you see is the ceiling, dark and
 651    receding.
 652  
 653    The water fills the chamber. It does not stop.
 654  end phrase
 655  
 656  define phrase spray-exposure
 657    A hiss from the cracked pipes overhead. Something cold and chemical
 658    settles on your skin, your eyes, your tongue. You blink. The letters on
 659    the sign shift. The walls flicker like a signal losing coherence.
 660  
 661    When your vision steadies, the world has not returned to normal. It has
 662    gone further.
 663  end phrase
 664  
 665  define phrase basin-touch
 666    You reach in. It reaches back.
 667  
 668    The light expands. It fills your hands, your arms, your chest. You feel it
 669    behind your eyes — not heat, not cold, but recognition. The building above
 670    you does not exist. The rooms, the corridors, the instruments — they were
 671    always this. Membrane and bone and light, folded into shapes you could
 672    almost name.
 673  
 674    You understand now. You have always been the patient. The treatment is
 675    complete.
 676  end phrase
